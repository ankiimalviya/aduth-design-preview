Privacy Policy

Updated 3 October 2026 · Aduth AI Media

This notice covers the ULE early-access website operated by Aduth AI Media. Questions or data requests: Contact Aduth.

What you share

When you request early access, we collect your name, email address, area of interest, and agreement to be contacted. You may also choose to share a business or website and an outreach challenge. Please do not include passwords, payment details, sensitive personal information, or client contact lists.

Why we use it

We use these details to understand interest in ULE, review potential testing use cases, and contact you about early access or the collaboration interest you select. Joining does not subscribe you to unrelated marketing.

Services involved

Cloudflare hosts the website, stores signup entries in D1, and provides Turnstile spam protection. Turnstile processes technical browser and network information, including IP addresses, to check submissions. The application does not store raw IP addresses in its waitlist table.

When notification email is enabled, Resend processes the submitted details to send an internal notification to our Gmail inbox. Google processes that email as the inbox provider. These services may process data outside your country. Website fonts are loaded from CDNFonts, which receives normal connection information such as your IP address. LinkedIn receives information if you choose to follow its external link.

Storage and access

Waitlist entries are not published or available through a public list or search endpoint. They are accessible to the site operator and necessary service providers. We do not sell signup details. Our application is configured to remove waitlist records after 12 months. Email copies and provider logs or backups are managed separately and may remain for their providers' retention periods; contact us if you want your entry and related messages removed.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the details you submitted, or withdraw permission for future contact, by emailing us from the address you used. We may ask for enough information to verify that the request is yours. Withdrawing contact permission does not undo messages already sent.

Cookies and browser storage

This website does not add advertising analytics or store signup details in browser storage. Cloudflare security services may use technical signals or storage needed to protect the service. Read Cloudflare's privacy policy for its processing.

Changes

We will update this page when the signup process or services change. If ULE introduces product accounts, payments, or additional data collection, those features will need their own updated notices.
