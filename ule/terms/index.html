Updated 3 October 2026 · Aduth AI Media

These terms cover the ULE information page and early-access waitlist operated by Aduth AI Media. Contact: Contact Aduth.

What you are requesting

ULE is an AI sales workspace in development. The page describes its intended direction; it does not mean every feature is available today. Submitting the form requests consideration for early testing or the collaboration interest you select.

No purchase or guaranteed invitation

Joining this waitlist is free and does not start a paid subscription. There is no guaranteed invitation, launch date, feature set, or business outcome. Any future paid service would require separate information and your agreement before you are charged.

Testing and collaboration

An invitation may include a limited feature set and a request for feedback. Selecting sales collaboration does not create a partnership, employment relationship, commission agreement, or authority to represent Aduth AI Media. Any such arrangement must be agreed separately.

Responsible submissions

Use your own contact details or details you are authorised to provide. Do not submit someone else's personal information without permission, send spam, attempt to access other entries, or interfere with the website. Please keep feedback relevant and avoid confidential customer data.

Contact and privacy

We may contact you about your request and selected interest. You can withdraw permission by emailing us. The Privacy Policy explains what the form collects and how it is handled.

Availability and changes

The website, waitlist, and development plans may change or pause. We will describe actual access conditions when inviting testers. Nothing on this page promises leads, meetings, revenue, or uninterrupted service, or removes rights that apply under relevant law.
